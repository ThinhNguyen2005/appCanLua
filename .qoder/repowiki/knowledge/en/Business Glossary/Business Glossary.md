### Cân Lúa
- Definition：The app's product name (also written as Cân Lúa). A Kotlin/Jetpack Compose Android tool for rice farmers and traders in Vietnam's Mekong Delta, providing offline-first card management, market prices, AI-assisted agronomy advice, and seasonal analysis.
- Aliases：Cân Lúa app、CanLua

### FARMER / TRADER / GUEST
- Definition：The three user roles in Cân Lúa. FARMER and TRADER drive different AI prompt personas (Nông dân vs Thương lái) and different knowledge-base audiences (agronomy vs market docs); GUEST is the unauthenticated role. Role is stored on the user Profile entity.
- Aliases：roles、user roles

### Phiếu cân
- Definition：A weighing ticket / receipt generated by the app for recording rice harvest weigh-ins. Exported as PDF via `PdfExporter` and shared through Android's `ACTION_SEND` using a fixed `FileProvider` authority `${applicationId}.fileprovider`.
- Aliases：weighing ticket、ticket

### Vụ
- Definition：A rice growing season / crop cycle. Used as the grouping key for seasonal analytics and as the cache key for AI season-analysis results (session-scoped `seasonCache` keyed by `seasonSummary.hashCode()`).
- Aliases：crop season、growing season

### Bảng giá lúa hôm nay
- Definition：Today's rice price board — the list of varieties, min/max prices, 7-day average, trend, and freshness timestamp that is embedded into the AI system prompt so the assistant can answer price questions strictly from this data.
- Aliases：rice price board、price board

### Tài liệu khuyến nông nội bộ
- Definition：Internal agronomy knowledge base (loaded from `assets/agronomy_knowledge.json`) injected into the farmer-facing AI prompt as trusted reference material to prevent hallucinated pest/disease/fertilizer advice.
- Aliases：internal agronomy KB、knowledge base

### Tài liệu thị trường nội bộ
- Definition：Internal market/trader knowledge base (loaded from `assets/trader_knowledge.json`) injected into the trader-facing AI prompt as trusted reference material for purchasing/quality/logistics guidance.
- Aliases：internal market KB、trader knowledge base

### Đồng bằng sông Cửu Long
- Definition：The Mekong Delta region of Vietnam — the geographic scope of Cân Lúa's domain. AI prompts and UI copy are tailored to farmers and traders operating in ĐBSCL, using local units (công, giạ, bao 50kg) and terminology.
- Aliases：ĐBSCL、Mekong Delta
